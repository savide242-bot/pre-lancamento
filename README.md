# BabyBites — Official Pre-Launch Campaign

## Structure

- `index.html` — The main landing page with all 14 strategic sections and exact direct-response copy.
- `styles.css` — High-end styling with international aesthetic (cream, soft sage, deep forest green, micro-animations, phone mockup).
- `script.js` — Live real-time countdown to November 30, 2026, plus automatic media detection.
- `assets/videos/` — Place your app preview video here (e.g., `babybites-preview.mp4`).
- `assets/images/` — Place your app screenshots and mockups here.

## How to add your video

Simply place your video file into the folder:
`assets/videos/babybites-preview.mp4`

The page will automatically detect it and play it inside the iPhone mockup with seamless loop and sound muted. If no video is placed yet, the page automatically displays a high-end simulated app interface.

## How to add your app screenshots

You can place your app screenshots in `assets/images/` or share them directly in the chat, and we will integrate them into the feature showcase and mockup screens.
